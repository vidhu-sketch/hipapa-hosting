FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

From hosting setup to managing your emails, we're here to guide you every step of the way.

What is shared hosting and who is it for?

Shared hosting is a cost-effective solution where multiple websites share the same server. It’s perfect for small to medium websites, blogs, and startups looking to get online quickly and affordably.

Is there any limit on website traffic or bandwidth?

No – our shared hosting comes with unmetered and unlimited bandwidth options, so your website can handle traffic smoothly without unexpected overage fees.

Will I get an SSL certificate for free?

Yes, every shared hosting plan includes a free SSL certificate, ensuring your site is secure and trusted by visitors and search engines.

Can I create professional email addresses with this plan?

Yes, you can create custom professional email addresses using your domain name (e.g. info@yourdomain.com) with full webmail and mobile sync support.

Do I need technical knowledge to manage shared hosting?

Not at all. With user-friendly cPanel access, you can easily manage your website, emails, and databases with a simple dashboard – no coding or technical expertise required.
