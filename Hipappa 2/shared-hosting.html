Web Hosting FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our Shared Web Hosting services.

What is shared web hosting and is it suitable for my website?

Shared web hosting allows multiple websites to utilize shared server resources. It is the most cost-effective and user-friendly choice for blogs, small business sites, and personal portfolios.

Do you provide cPanel control panel with shared hosting?

Yes! All our Linux shared web hosting plans come pre-installed with cPanel, allowing you to easily manage domain names, email accounts, and database files.

How long does it take to activate my hosting account?

Instant Activation! As soon as your order payment is processed, your hosting account credentials are sent to your email inbox immediately.

Can I upgrade to VPS hosting later if my traffic grows?

Yes! You can seamlessly upgrade from Shared Hosting to Managed VPS or Dedicated Servers anytime with zero data loss or website downtime.
