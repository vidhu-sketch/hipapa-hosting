FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Whether you're a beginner or a developer, we've got the answers to make your WordPress hosting smooth and stress-free.

What makes WordPress hosting different from shared hosting?

WordPress hosting is optimized specifically for WordPress sites, offering better speed, security, and features like automatic updates and staging environments that standard shared hosting may not include.

Is the environment really managed? What does that mean?

Yes, it's fully managed – we handle server-level updates, performance tuning, backups, and security patches so you can focus solely on your website.

Do I need to install a CDN separately?

No, our WordPress hosting comes with an integrated LiteSpeed-powered CDN, already optimized for WordPress, improving global site performance.

What is WP-CLI and who is it for?

WP-CLI is a command-line interface for developers to manage WordPress installations – perfect for advanced users who prefer faster control over plugins, themes, updates, and more.

Can I test changes before going live?

Yes! Use our 1-click staging feature to test updates, plugins, or redesigns in a safe environment before pushing them live.
