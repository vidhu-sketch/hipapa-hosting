FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clarity on setup, control, and capabilities – everything you need to know about VPS Hosting.

What is VPS hosting, and how is it different from shared hosting?

VPS (Virtual Private Server) hosting gives you dedicated resources and full control over your environment, unlike shared hosting where server space and resources are shared with others.

Do I get full root access and SSH control with my VPS?

Yes. All VPS plans include full root access and SSH, giving you complete control over the server’s configuration, applications, and security.

Can I choose the operating system for my VPS?

Absolutely. You can select from a wide range of Linux distributions (like Ubuntu, CentOS, AlmaLinux) or even opt for Windows OS – depending on your project requirements.

How easily can I scale my VPS resources?

You can scale your VPS resources like RAM, CPU, or storage instantly without downtime – perfect for growing websites and evolving applications.

Is my VPS protected against DDoS attacks?

Yes. All VPS hosting plans include DDoS protection, ensuring your server stays online and secure against common cyber threats.
